Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. Who operates Fishing Hub?

Fishing Hub is a free, non-commercial hobby project operated for entertainment and a small community. For privacy questions or requests, contact fishingsyshub@gmail.com.

2. What data is processed?

When you use Twitch login, Fishing Hub may process your Twitch user ID, login name, display name and profile image. The game also stores data associated with your Twitch account, such as fishing results, inventory, progression, Fishdex progress, statistics, settings, trades/gifts and other game state needed to provide the game.

Selected Twitch usernames and game statistics can appear on in-game leaderboards displayed by the streamer’s local game server.

If a streamer connects their Twitch account in the Broadcaster Admin panel, Fishing Hub stores the streamer's Twitch user ID, login name, the Twitch permissions they granted (moderation:read, channel:read:vips and channel:read:subscriptions), and an OAuth refresh token. This lets Fishing Hub check whether viewers in that streamer's chat are moderators, VIPs or subscribers so the game can apply bonuses. For this check, Fishing Hub looks up viewers' Twitch user IDs and their moderator, VIP and subscriber status in that channel. These lookups are cached for up to 7 days (user IDs) and 5 minutes (results) and are not used for anything else.

Technical information such as IP addresses may be processed by Twitch during sign-in and by the streamer’s operating system/network provider. In this local edition, the Fishingbot web server accepts connections only from the streamer’s own computer; external viewer access is disabled.

Reports and ideas: if you use the Report a problem or Submit an idea form, Fishing Hub processes the title and text you enter, the optional PNG screenshot you attach, the date and time the report was sent and, unless you tick "Send anonymously", your Twitch login name. It is emailed to the Fishing Hub inbox (fishingsyshub@gmail.com). The screenshot is attached to that email only. Fishing Hub does not store it on the website or in its database, and does not log the content. Please do not put personal information about yourself or other people in a report or an idea.

The forms limit how many reports and ideas one visitor can send in an hour. This limit is kept in the server's memory only and is not stored.

3. Cookies

Fishing Hub uses a session cookie to keep a logged-in user authenticated and a short-lived OAuth state cookie during Twitch login, and a broadcaster admin session cookie for streamers who unlock the Broadcaster Admin panel. These are used to provide the requested service and security features. Fishing Hub does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies.

4. Why is the data processed?

Data is processed to authenticate users with Twitch, check moderator, VIP and subscriber status in a connected streamer's chat so the game can apply bonuses, provide and save the fishing game, display requested leaderboards, prevent abuse and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

5. Service providers

In the self-hosted desktop edition, game data is stored in a local SQLite database on the streamer’s computer. The local web server listens only on the streamer’s own computer; external viewer sharing is disabled in this release. Twitch provides login and Twitch-related functionality, and its own privacy information applies.

Reports and ideas are sent by email through Resend, an email delivery service, which processes each report only to deliver it to the Fishing Hub inbox.

6. Retention and deletion

Game data is retained in the streamer's local database until it is deleted through an available user deletion control or the streamer deletes/removes the database. A streamer's Twitch connection is kept until they disconnect it; disconnecting removes the stored token and asks Twitch to revoke it. Cached Twitch lookups expire automatically after at most 7 days (user IDs) and 5 minutes (results). You can request deletion of data associated with your Twitch account by using the website's authenticated “Delete my data” function where available, or by contacting the streamer who operates the local game server. Deletion cannot be undone. Backups made by the streamer may persist until the streamer deletes them.

Reports and ideas are kept in the Fishing Hub email inbox until Fishing Hub deletes them. A screenshot exists only as an email attachment and is not kept on the website or in the database.

7. Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection where applicable, and data portability where applicable. You may also complain to a competent data protection supervisory authority.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the service or its data processing changes. The current version is published on this page.
