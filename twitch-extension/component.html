    Legal & Extension policy

    FREE SOFTWARE
    01 / NON-MONETIZED SOFTWARE

    Free to use

    Fishingbot Twitch Extension is provided free of charge. It is a non-monetized community feature: this Extension does not sell subscriptions, paid access, paid upgrades, virtual currency or in-Extension purchases. No payment is required to open the World Map or use its included features.

    02 / IDENTITY AND PLAYER DATA

    Account linking is optional

    The public map shell can load without sharing your identity. To view private player progress or make account changes, use Twitch's identity-sharing prompt and approve it. Fishingbot uses the authenticated Twitch identity to request the matching player profile; it must not let one viewer retrieve another viewer's private inventory or settings.

    Private account requests are sent over HTTPS to the Fishingbot relay and are handled by the connected broadcaster's Fishingbot service. Do not share your Twitch login, access token or account credentials with anyone.

    03 / SERVICE AND TRADEMARKS

    Third-party platform notice

    Fishingbot is an independent community project and is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Twitch. Twitch names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Availability depends on Twitch Extension services, the Fishingbot relay and the broadcaster's local application.

    04 / ACCEPTABLE USE

    Use the Extension responsibly

    Do not attempt to bypass access controls, impersonate another player, disrupt the relay or use the Extension to harass other viewers. Game features, catch chances and available actions are controlled by the current Fishingbot build and broadcaster configuration.
